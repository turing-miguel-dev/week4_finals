## 1. Project Overview

-   **Project Name:** Deals Dough Hub Pizza House
-   **Project Type:** Online Pizza Ordering Website
-   **Purpose:** To create a simple and user-friendly website where
    customers can explore the menu, view food products, add items to
    their cart, and prepare their orders.

The website uses a warm and attractive pizza-themed design. It focuses
on providing customers with an easy way to browse food products and
place orders.

## 2. Technologies Used

-   **HTML** -- Creates the structure of each webpage.
-   **CSS** -- Handles the website's design, colors, layout, and
    responsiveness.
-   **JavaScript** -- Handles cart functions, notifications, and
    interactive features.
-   **Google Fonts** -- Uses Fraunces and Nunito Sans for typography.

## 3. Website Pages

The website contains five main pages:

### 1. Home Page (`index.html`)

-   Displays the Deals Dough Hub brand.
-   Shows featured pizza images using an image slider.
-   Introduces the available food categories.
-   Provides quick links to the menu and contact page.
-   Displays the shopping cart button.

### 2. About Page (`about.html`)

-   Introduces the pizza business.
-   Explains the company's story and values.
-   Shows information about fresh ingredients and food quality.
-   Displays opening hours and business contact information.

### 3. Orders Page (`orders.html`)

-   Displays the available food products.
-   Organizes products into different categories:
    -   Pizza for Sharing
    -   Pasta for Me
    -   Solo Meal
    -   Sides and Desserts
-   Shows product names, descriptions, and prices.
-   Allows customers to add products to their cart.

### 4. Cart Page (`cart.html`)

-   Displays the selected products.
-   Shows the total price of the order.
-   Allows customers to clear their cart.
-   Provides a checkout form for customer information.
-   Allows customers to select pickup or delivery.
-   Prepares the order through the customer's email application.

### 5. Contact Page (`contact.html`)

-   Provides a contact form.
-   Allows customers to send questions and feedback.
-   Displays the business address, phone number, and opening hours.
